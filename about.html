About ToolNest

ToolNest is a free collection of online tools and calculators. It was built around a simple idea: everyday calculations and small tasks should be quick, free, and private — no sign-up, no downloads, no waiting.

What you can do here

ToolNest offers 58 tools, all free to use:

  • Calculators: Age Calculator, BMI Calculator, Percentage Calculator, Loan Calculator, Tip Calculator, Date Calculator, Compound Interest Calculator, Discount Calculator, Salary & Hourly Converter, Speed / Distance / Time Calculator, Negative Number Calculator, Negative Marking Calculator, GPA Calculator, Body Fat Calculator, Mortgage Calculator, Simple Interest Calculator, Investment Calculator, Retirement Calculator, Savings Goal Calculator, Inflation Calculator, VAT / Sales Tax Calculator, Split Bill Calculator, Calorie Calculator, Ideal Weight Calculator, Pregnancy Due Date Calculator, Ovulation Calculator, Target Heart Rate Calculator, Fraction Calculator, Scientific Calculator, Time Calculator, Grade Calculator, CGPA to Percentage Converter and Area Calculator.
  • Everyday helpers: Unit Converter (length, weight and temperature), Word Counter, Password Generator, QR Code Generator, Stopwatch, Currency Converter, Countdown Timer, Random Number Generator, Text Case Converter, Color Palette Generator, Pomodoro Timer, Lorem Ipsum Generator, Sleep Calculator, Roman Numeral Converter, Time Zone Converter, Fuel Cost Calculator, Water Intake Calculator and Number to Words Converter.
  • Fun tools: Love Calculator, Coin Flip, Dice Roller, Random Name Generator, Rock Paper Scissors, a Random Joke Generator and a Fun Fact Generator.

Private by design

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type — your date of birth, your text, your passwords — is uploaded to any server or stored anywhere. When you close the page, it is gone. See our Privacy Policy for details.
