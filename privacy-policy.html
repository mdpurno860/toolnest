Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

ToolNest respects your privacy. This page explains what the site does — and does not do — with your information.

No data is collected

ToolNest does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All tools run entirely in your browser: anything you type into a calculator or tool (for example your date of birth, text, or a generated password) stays on your device and is never sent to any server.

No accounts

There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no login on ToolNest. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal detail to use the tools.

Cookies

ToolNest itself does not set any cookies and does not use local storage to track you. However, third-party advertising on this site (Google AdSense) may use cookies to show ads. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services

This site loads a font from Google Fonts and a QR code library from cdnjs. When your browser loads these resources, those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as described in their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at mpurno860@gmail.com.
